Research Output

Publications

DEG-MobNet: Lightweight Multi-View Structural MRI Learning for Early Diagnosis of Alzheimer’s Disease

Polash Chandra Das, Jiyun Li, Han Xia, et al. · December 1–4, 2026 · IEEE International Conference on Bioinformatics and Biomedicine (BIBM) · CCF-B · Accepted

  • A lightweight multi-view structural MRI framework for Alzheimer’s disease diagnosis and MCI progression prediction using axial, coronal, and sagittal anatomical representations.
  • Uses a shared lightweight encoder with dilated convolution, ECA, GeM pooling, and subject-level attention pooling to reduce computational cost while preserving discriminative anatomical features.
  • Integrates MRI representations with MMSE, CDR-SB, and APOE ε4 clinical information and evaluates four ADNI classification tasks, including pMCI vs. sMCI.

PIACM: Prototype-Induced Axis-Consensus Mamba Network for Text-Guided 3D Medical Image Segmentation

Jiabao Zhao, Han Xia, Ren Xia, Polash Chandra Das, Yan Gong, Jiyun Li, and Chen Qian · December 1–4, 2026 · IEEE International Conference on Bioinformatics and Biomedicine (BIBM) · CCF-B · Accepted

  • A text-guided Mamba framework for 3D medical image segmentation.
  • Develops a Semantic-Guided Voxel Adapter (SGVA) to align voxel features with text-derived anatomical semantic prototypes.
  • Introduces Axis-Consensus Local-Frequency Mamba (ACLF-Mamba) to adaptively model coronal, sagittal, and axial volumetric dependencies.
  • Evaluates the framework on four public CT/MRI datasets, with strong anatomical segmentation and boundary preservation performance.

DBANet: Dual-Branch Adaptive Network with Topology-Aware Token Transport for Alzheimer’s Disease Staging from Structural MRI

Yue Li, Sajad Ul Haq, Polash Chandra Das, et al. · August 22–25, 2026 · The 9th Chinese Conference on Pattern Recognition and Computer Vision (PRCV) · Harbin, China · Paper ID: 2578 · CCF-C · Accepted

  • A dual-branch framework combining a pretrained Swin Transformer Tiny and Graph Attention Network (GAT) for Alzheimer’s disease staging from structural MRI.
  • Proposes a Topology-Aware Token Transport (TATT) module with spatially aligned bidirectional cross-attention and learnable gating, followed by adaptive soft-attention fusion for sample-specific integration of visual and structural features.
  • Evaluated on ADNI using strict subject-level partitioning.
