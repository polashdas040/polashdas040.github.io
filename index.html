ABOUT

Research with a practical direction.

My interests sit at the intersection of artificial intelligence, medical imaging, and efficient deep learning.

I am especially interested in models that can capture clinically meaningful structure while remaining computationally efficient. My work explores structural MRI analysis, multi-view representation learning, subject-level modeling, and methods for Alzheimer's disease diagnosis.

Beyond medical imaging, I also explore modern AI systems including multimodal learning, time-series modeling, retrieval-augmented generation, and agentic workflows.

FEATURED RESEARCH

Selected research directions.

Current work centers on efficient representation learning and anatomy-aware modeling from structural MRI.

01
Medical AI Structural MRI

SCAH-MobNet

Lightweight Statistical–Contrast Recalibration and Anatomy-Aware Hierarchical Learning for Alzheimer's Disease Diagnosis.

A resource-efficient multi-view framework that learns from ROI-guided axial, coronal, and sagittal MRI slices using a shared lightweight encoder, statistical–contrast recalibration, and anatomy-aware subject-level pooling.

Shared multi-view encoder Anatomy-aware pooling Efficient inference Subject-level MRI learning

02
Longitudinal MRI Research Direction

NeuroGenesis-MRI

Longitudinal brain modeling for disease-progression-aware representation learning.

Exploring longitudinal MRI representations that capture subject-specific anatomical change over time and support progression modeling in Alzheimer's disease research.
